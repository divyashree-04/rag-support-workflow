# **BrightCart Support Knowledge Base**

TITLE: Return policy for physical items

You can return most physical items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and include the receipt or order number. Final-sale items, personalized items, and opened hygiene products (such as candles that have been burned or opened personal-care items) cannot be returned.

Keywords: return, send back, money back, refund eligibility, 30 days

TITLE: How to start a return

Log in to your BrightCart account, open Order History, choose the order, and click Start Return. You will receive a prepaid return label by email within 1 business day. Pack the item securely and drop it off at any partner courier location.

Keywords: return label, start return, how to return, prepaid label

TITLE: Refund timing

Once the returned item arrives at our warehouse, we inspect it within 2 business days. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method and usually appear within 5 to 7 business days. Original shipping fees are not refunded unless the return is due to our error.

Keywords: how long refund, refund time, money back, when will I get my refund

TITLE: Refunds for digital products

Digital products such as e-books, templates, and software licenses can be refunded within 7 days of purchase if they have not been downloaded or activated. Once a digital product is downloaded or activated, it is not refundable. If a file is corrupted or does not work, contact support and we will fix it or refund you.

Keywords: digital refund, ebook, download, software, license, template

TITLE: Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. To swap an item for a different size or color, return the original item for a refund and place a new order. To make sure the new item is in stock, place the new order first and then start the return.

Keywords: exchange, swap, different size, different color, replace

TITLE: Damaged or defective items

If your item arrives damaged or defective, contact support within 14 days of delivery with your order number and a photo of the problem. We will send a free replacement or give a full refund, including original shipping fees. You do not need to return items that cost less than 20 dollars.

Keywords: broken, damaged, defective, faulty, arrived broken, wrong item

TITLE: Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order for free until it enters the packing stage, which usually happens within 2 hours of purchase. To cancel, go to Order History and click Cancel Order. If the Cancel button is missing, the order has already been packed, and you will need to wait for delivery and then start a return.

Keywords: cancel, cancellation, stop my order, undo order

TITLE: Changing your shipping address

You can change the shipping address from Order History until the order enters the packing stage. After that, we cannot change the address, because the shipping label has already been printed. If the package is sent to a wrong address that you entered, we cannot cover the cost of reshipping.

Keywords: change address, wrong address, update delivery address, move

TITLE: Standard shipping times

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the United States after the order is packed. Orders placed before 2 PM Eastern Time on a business day are packed the same day. Orders placed on weekends or holidays are packed on the next business day.

Keywords: delivery time, how long shipping, when will it arrive, standard delivery

TITLE: Express shipping

Express shipping delivers in 1 to 2 business days within the United States and costs 14.99 dollars. Express shipping is not available for oversized items such as furniture and large rugs. Express orders must be placed before 12 PM Eastern Time to be packed the same day.

Keywords: fast shipping, overnight, rush, expedited, next day

TITLE: Shipping costs and free shipping

Standard shipping costs 5.99 dollars. Orders of 50 dollars or more qualify for free standard shipping in the United States. The free shipping threshold is calculated after discounts are applied.

Keywords: shipping fee, free shipping, delivery cost, minimum order

TITLE: International shipping

We currently ship to the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and Australia only. International orders take 7 to 14 business days. Customers are responsible for any customs duties or import taxes charged by their country.

Keywords: ship abroad, overseas, outside US, international delivery, customs, other countries

TITLE: Tracking your order

Once your order ships, you will receive an email with a tracking number and a tracking link. You can also find tracking details in Order History. Tracking updates may take up to 24 hours to appear after the label is created.

Keywords: track, where is my order, tracking number, order status

TITLE: Lost or delayed packages

If your tracking has not updated for 5 business days or the package is more than 7 business days late, contact support. We will open an investigation with the carrier and either resend the order or refund you within 3 business days of confirming the loss. Please check with neighbors and your building's front desk first.

Keywords: lost package, missing, not delivered, late delivery, stolen package

TITLE: Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. We do not accept checks, cash on delivery, or cryptocurrency. You can pay with only one payment method per order.

Keywords: pay, credit card, debit card, paypal, apple pay, payment options

TITLE: Payment declined or failed

If your payment is declined, first check that your card details and billing address are correct. Banks sometimes block online purchases, so contacting your bank can help. If you see a charge but no order confirmation, wait 24 hours, because pending charges usually drop off automatically.

Keywords: card declined, payment error, charged twice, payment failed, pending charge

TITLE: Promo codes and discounts

Enter your promo code at checkout in the Discount Code box. Only one promo code can be used per order, and codes cannot be combined with other offers. Promo codes cannot be applied after an order is placed.

Keywords: coupon, discount code, voucher, promotion, apply code

TITLE: Price adjustments

If an item you bought goes on sale within 14 days of your purchase, contact support and we will refund the difference. Price adjustments do not apply to clearance items, final-sale items, or limited-time flash sales.

Keywords: price drop, price match, sale after purchase, cheaper

TITLE: Creating an account and resetting your password

To create an account, click Sign Up and enter your email and a password. To reset a forgotten password, click Forgot Password on the login page and enter your email. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes, and you should check your spam folder if you do not see it.

Keywords: login, sign up, forgot password, reset password, can't log in

TITLE: Deleting your account and your data

You can request deletion of your account and personal data by emailing privacy@brightcart.example from your registered email address. We complete deletion requests within 30 days. Order records required for tax and legal purposes are kept for up to 7 years in a limited form.

Keywords: delete account, privacy, personal data, remove my data, GDPR

TITLE: Product warranty

Electronics and appliances sold by BrightCart include a 1-year limited warranty that covers manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage, or normal wear and tear. To make a claim, contact support with your order number and a description of the problem.

Keywords: warranty, guarantee, repair, covered, manufacturing defect

TITLE: BrightCart Rewards program

You earn 1 point for every dollar spent, and 100 points can be redeemed for 5 dollars off a future order. Points expire 12 months after they are earned. Points are not awarded on shipping fees, taxes, or gift-wrap charges, and they are removed if an order is refunded.

Keywords: rewards, points, loyalty, redeem points, membership

TITLE: Contacting support

Our support team is available by email at help@brightcart.example and by live chat on our website, Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern Time. We respond to emails within 24 hours on business days. We do not offer phone support at this time.

Keywords: contact, customer service, support hours, email support, live chat, phone number

TITLE: Bulk and business orders

Orders of 25 or more units of the same item qualify for business pricing. To request a quote, email business@brightcart.example with the product names, quantities, and delivery location. Quotes are sent within 2 business days.

Keywords: wholesale, bulk, business order, corporate, large quantity

TITLE: Gift wrapping and gift receipts

Gift wrapping is available at checkout for 3.99 dollars per item. You can also select the Gift Receipt option, which hides prices on the packing slip. Gift recipients can start a return using the order number from the gift receipt, and the refund goes to the original purchaser as store credit.

Keywords: gift, gift wrap, present, gift receipt, birthday
